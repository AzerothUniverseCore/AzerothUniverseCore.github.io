## What is AzerothUniverseCore / UniverseEmu?

UniverseEmu is a TrinityCore-based (3.3.5a) server framework powering the Azeroth Universe project. It brings Cataclysm and Mists of Pandaria-era content and systems on top of a WotLK 3.3.5a client, along with a large set of custom systems (progression, dungeons/raids, customization, mounts & battle pets, and more — see the [project README](https://github.com/AzerothUniverseCore/UniverseEmu) for the full list).

It is open source. Community involvement is welcome — see [CONTRIBUTING.md](https://github.com/AzerothUniverseCore/UniverseEmu/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md) if you'd like to submit code, scripts, or content.
